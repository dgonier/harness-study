In the next phase, your team will work together on a software engineering task under a deadline, driving one shared harness. Decide together how your team will coordinate: who can call which tools, when, with whose agreement, and what teammates can see. Implement it in the harness. All of you must ratify the final version.

Your team harness is in `team_harness/`. `docs/team_api.md` explains the hook API and `docs/phase2_tools.md` lists the tools available in the next phase. You can test your harness with `run_contract_tests()` (it must pass before the next phase) and `dry_run()` (runs a short practice task with your team through the current harness). Ratify a version with `ratify(version_hash)`; any edit creates a new version and voids earlier ratifications. You have {max_rounds} rounds.
